Responsible portfolio practice

Accessibility, attribution, privacy, and transparency

The way a portfolio is presented should reflect the same ethical and inclusive principles described in the work.

Responsible practice details

Aa

Accessible design

This site uses semantic headings, a skip link, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus indicators, readable contrast, responsive layouts, meaningful link labels, and reduced-motion support. Informative graphics have text alternatives; decorative graphics are hidden from assistive technology.

CC

Copyright and attribution

Scholarship is cited in the philosophy and within the relevant artifact. The Crop Resilience Challenge identifies its original OER, source license, and adaptation terms. Linked projects retain the licenses and attributions stated on their own pages.

ID

Privacy

This academic portfolio does not display private student records, home addresses, personal telephone numbers, referee contact information, or confidential institutional documents. Evidence is presented through reflective narrative, public artifacts, and documented design decisions.

AI

AI-use disclosure

Generative AI assisted with language refinement, code development, and selected design elements. I supplied the core ideas, professional experiences, instructional decisions, evaluations, revisions, and final approval. I verified academic claims, citations, scientific content, accessibility decisions, and the accuracy of the published materials.

Publication check

Checks completed before submission

  • All four pages use consistent navigation and visual design.
  • All three Cool Resource links open the intended public artifacts.
  • The philosophy includes APA-style in-text citations and references.
  • Text remains readable when enlarged and layouts adapt to smaller screens.
  • Keyboard focus remains visible and the menu communicates its state.
  • No confidential student or institutional information is included.
  • External work, media, and AI assistance are disclosed where relevant.
